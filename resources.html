Resources for Johannesburg and Gauteng. This is a prototype — always verify current contact details before relying on them in an emergency.

SAFETY FIRST

Are you in immediate danger?

If you are in immediate danger, move to a safer place if you can and contact emergency services or someone you trust.

01 / EMERGENCY CONTACTS

Call for help
right now.

These are general emergency numbers. Verify them locally before you need them.

South African Police Service (SAPS)

For immediate police assistance in any emergency.

10111 Available 24/7 nationwide

Emergency Medical Services

For medical emergencies and ambulance services.

10177 Available 24/7 nationwide

GBV Command Centre

National 24-hour helpline for gender-based violence. Call, SMS or WhatsApp.

0800 428 428 Free call · 24/7 · SMS 'Help' to 31531

Childline South Africa

Support for children in distress or danger. Free and confidential.

116 Free call · Available 24/7
02 / SHELTERS

Safe places
to stay.

Shelters provide temporary accommodation, counselling and support. Availability varies — contact directly to confirm space and services.

SHELTER · JOHANNESBURG

Usindiso Ministries

Provides shelter, food, trauma counselling and skills development for women and children affected by GBV.

Johannesburg CBD 011 336 3302
SHELTER · JOHANNESBURG

POWA (People Opposing Women Abuse)

Offers a safe shelter, legal support, counselling and advocacy for women experiencing abuse.

Johannesburg 011 642 4345
SHELTER · GAUTENG

Saartjie Baartman Centre

A multi-service centre providing emergency shelter, legal aid and long-term support for survivors of domestic violence.

Gauteng Verify current number locally
SHELTER · GAUTENG

Huis Maranata

Provides short-term shelter and counselling for women and children fleeing domestic violence situations.

Gauteng Verify current number locally
03 / NGOs & SUPPORT ORGANISATIONS

Professional
support & counselling.

These organisations can offer counselling, legal information, referrals and other forms of support.

COUNSELLING & LEGAL

Rape Crisis Cape Town Trust

Provides counselling, legal support and advocacy for survivors of sexual violence. Offers a national helpline.

rapecrisis.org.za 021 447 9762
COUNSELLING

SAPS Family Violence, Child Protection & Sexual Offences Unit

Specialised police unit for GBV, child abuse and sexual offences. Available at most SAPS stations.

10111 (emergency) Most SAPS stations
LEGAL AID

Legal Aid South Africa

Provides free legal services to people who cannot afford a lawyer, including domestic violence matters.

legal-aid.co.za 0800 110 110
COUNSELLING & SUPPORT

LifeLine South Africa

Offers 24-hour counselling, crisis intervention and referral services for people in distress.

0861 322 322 Branches nationwide
